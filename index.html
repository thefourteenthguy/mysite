How I walk

Something I've noticed is that I have three different styles of walking depending on how I'm feeling. I'm not sure if anyone else does this, given that it is hard to notice, but I find it interesting, so I'll go over it.

Diagram of the foot for reference


Relaxed

When relaxed/bored, my heel touches the ground first. I've been told that this is considered the "normal" way to walk

Motivated

When motivated, I walk so that the section of my foot in between my little toe and instep hits the ground first. It's worth noting that my "neutral" walk is near perfectly in between my motivated and relaxed walk.

Excited

When excited, my toes touch the ground first. They all touch the ground at roughly the same time. If I walked without favouring a side, my big toe would hit the ground first, as it is further forward, but in general, I favour the outside of my foot. Often, parts of my foot do not touch the ground at all when in this walk.
